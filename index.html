Who we are

Born in the village. Built with the village.

VARRAT — the Voluntary Association for Rural Reconstruction & Appropriate Technology — began as a simple attempt to stand by poor and disadvantaged families in distress. It has grown into a movement working with communities on gender rights, social justice, health and livelihoods.

Voices from the villages

A few of the people whose villages changed, and what changed for them.

Sunita, 34
Batighar
Her self-help group opened Batighar’s first group savings account. Now neighbours borrow from it instead of moneylenders.
Priya, 19
Kendrapara
The first girl in her family to finish school. Every evening she now tutors twelve younger children.
Ramesh, 52
Mahakalpara
A cyclone task-force volunteer. Before every storm season he walks the village with a loudspeaker and a list of who needs help leaving.
Lata, 41
Jajpur
Started a tailoring unit with her self-help group. It now employs women from three neighbouring hamlets.
Gopal, 67
Kendrapara
Lives alone. Each week a volunteer visits for a health check and a long chat.
Anjali, 28
Keonjhar
A community health worker. She knows which hamlets the ambulance can’t reach in the rains — and plans around them.
Meera, 23
Balasore
Leads a youth club that plants trees along the coast every monsoon.
